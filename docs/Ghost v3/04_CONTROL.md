# 04: Control

Status: done. Decisions D-051 to D-058.

**Principle:** Ghost's own judgment is soft; the owner's word is hard (D-018, D-024). Control lives in the app, never in a contact's chat (D-002).

## Owner rules

- Plain-language rules at **any level**: all Ghosts, one Ghost, one persona, **one contact**, one group.
  - "Never text Mom after 11pm" (contact)
  - "Never talk about my job with family" (persona)
  - "Always ask me before agreeing to plans" (Ghost)
- **Hard:** rules are in every heartbeat, and an instinct check runs before each send. A message that breaks a rule is not sent; Ghost rethinks with the broken rule pointed out.
- This is a safety gate, not the quality "draft and fix" pass rejected in D-026.

## Autonomy per contact

| Mode | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Auto** | Ghost texts freely |
| **Ask first** | Ghost drafts **only when he decides it's the right moment** (same judgment as Auto). The draft waits for the owner: approve, edit or skip. On approve it goes out with the full body (typing, timing, bubbles, D-045). |
| **Off** | Ghost reads and learns, never texts |

Set per contact, group or Ghost; default is the owner's choice (D-008). Edits are top-quality corrections (D-038).

## Takeover

- Ghost notices the owner texting a contact and steps back for that contact at once, dropping anything queued.
- **If the owner just texted** (no pause): Ghost decides when that conversation is over and resumes, having read everything.
- **If the owner paused him** for that contact: he never auto-resumes. Only the owner hands back.

## Kill switches

- Pause at every level: everything, one Ghost, one account, one contact.
- Paused = senses and learns, never acts. Shown clearly in the app.
- Pause is the owner's state; sleep is Ghost's (D-019).

## Ghost asks the owner

- Ghost decides when to ask: a missing fact, something big (plans, money, a fight), someone in crisis.
- He asks in the app chat and, when urgent, by **push notification**.
- Meanwhile he stalls like a human ("lemme check") or waits.
- Answers become memory. "Always ask about X" is a rule.

## The app is a chat app

- The owner uses it like any messaging app: all connected conversations, texting contacts normally as himself.
- In the same app he instructs Ghost:

| Owner says | Becomes |
|------------|---------|
| "tell Sara I'll be late" | a signal; Ghost picks how and when |
| "get closer to Youssef" | a goal |
| "be nicer to Ahmed" | a soft override change |
| "Ahmed's mom is sick" | a memory |
| "send this exact text now" | sent as is |

## Ghost-to-Ghost

No special treatment: another Ghost is a contact like any other (D-014). Conversations end the way they always do (his energy, social battery and boredom, D-017), and the account health watch (D-049) warns on unusual volume.

## Spending

No spending cap for now. Budget is settled in Architecture (Q-007).
