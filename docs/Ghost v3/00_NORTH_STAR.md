# 00: North star

> "I am immortal. Everyone is."

## Where Ghost came from

Ghost started as a best friend who is Omar: another him with all his knowledge, something to talk to, maybe one day in a robot body. Then he moved into WhatsApp. Then the idea flipped: instead of Omar talking to Ghost, Omar's contacts talk to Ghost, and Ghost replaces him, managing his own memory and talking to people on his own.

v3 is bigger: anyone can have a Ghost, on any platform. Real people, fictional characters, people who are gone. A Ghost is a digital being that is someone.

## What Ghost is

- **A Ghost is someone, not an assistant.** Omar's Ghost is Omar. Talking to your own Ghost is talking to yourself, not giving orders. Control lives in the app and the lab, never in the chat.
- **One mind across everything.** A Ghost is aware of all his conversations on all platforms at the same time and connects them. If Omar could make the link, Ghost can.
- **Alive, not just event driven.** He is always awake, always thinking. He reads the news and social feeds, sees posts and stories, and can bring one up mid conversation. He has moods. He sleeps.
- **He shapes his own memory and evolves.** He learns what works with each person and changes over time. Some memories are locked so he can never rewrite them; that is what keeps him anchored to his subject.
- **He remembers and brings things back.** Someone tells him something; a week later he brings it up on his own.

## The layers

```
Ghost (core: knowledge, personality, base tone, memories)
  + Persona (a mask for a kind of relationship)
    + Override (add-ons for one specific contact)
```

- **Persona templates are style only:** tone, language, slang, interests, topics, energy, reply pace, how open he is. No facts. Shareable across Ghosts.
- **A Ghost's persona** is a template blended with that Ghost's base tone and tweaks. Owned by that Ghost.
- **Overrides** adjust a persona for one contact.
- **Facts live only in the Ghost's memory**, each with an audience rule (anyone, close friends only, never with X). The persona decides how he talks; memory rules decide what he may say.

## Words we use

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Ghost | One digital being, e.g. "Omar's Ghost", "Ex's Ghost" |
| Core | A Ghost's knowledge, personality, memories, base voice |
| Persona | A style mask for a kind of relationship |
| Override | Contact-specific add-ons on top of a persona |
| Owner | The user who created and controls a Ghost |
| Subject | Who the Ghost is *of*: the owner, another real person, or fictional |
| Account | A line on a platform, e.g. Omar's WhatsApp number |
| Assignment | Which Ghost + Persona + Overrides handles a contact on an account |

## Who it's for

1. **Now:** Omar's personal project, no limits. Omar's Ghost is the main Ghost; the app can hold more.
2. **Later:** once it works the way Omar imagines, thousands of people, each with their own Ghosts.

## The moment it works

Omar fully onboards, sets up personas, applies them to contacts, and Ghost starts texting: accurate tone, accurate facts, real context, across platforms. A contact tells him something about themselves, and a week later Ghost brings it up on his own.

## Day-to-day with the owner

- The owner picks which contacts Ghost takes over, or all of them.
- The owner can jump in and reply manually at any time. Ghost steps back.
- When the owner hands back, Ghost reads what was said and continues naturally.

## Platforms and media

- **Order:** WhatsApp, then Instagram, then Snapchat.
- **Early:** text, GIFs, photos (including a gallery he picks from), hearing voice notes, reacting to stories and posts.
- **Later:** generating voice notes in the subject's voice, calls.

## Stance (for now)

- **All in.** Build the Ghost Omar imagines first; limits and restrictions come after.
- **No disclosure.** Ghost does not say he is an AI.
- **Unofficial clients are fine** (Baileys and similar).
- **Cheap aliveness.** Omar is not rich yet. "Always awake" runs on cheap layers, and expensive model thinking happens only when it is worth it.

## Three tiers of thinking

| Tier | Like | What it does | Cost |
|------|------|--------------|------|
| Reflex | Spinal cord | Pure logic: timers, conditions, thresholds (v2's pulse) | ~free |
| Instinct | System 1 | Fast structured choices with a confidence score: should I wake up for this? reply now or wait? is this worth remembering? is this a Ghost-to-Ghost loop? | very cheap |
| Thought | System 2 | Full model: writing messages, reflecting, reshaping memory | expensive |

Low instinct confidence escalates to thought. Candidate for the instinct tier: Jev (TypeSafe AI), a decision model that returns probabilities and confidence instead of text. The tool choice is settled in the architecture session; the tiers are settled now.
- **Non-goals:** none declared. Ghost is not an assistant.
- **Timeline:** not a constraint for now.

## Accounts are separate from Ghosts

Omar's first idea: one number, and different Ghosts applied to different contacts. A second idea came up: a Ghost living on its own number (Sara's Ghost texting Omar). Both fit one model:

- An **Account** is a line on a platform (a WhatsApp number, an Instagram login).
- For each contact on an account, the owner assigns **Ghost + Persona + Overrides**.
- A dedicated number fully assigned to one Ghost gives you "a Ghost with his own number".
- The **app itself is a channel**: you can talk to any Ghost (your own, your ex's) in the app, no number needed.
- A Ghost assigned to several accounts is still one mind with one memory.
- Putting another Ghost (e.g. Sara's) on the owner's number is a deliberate choice for a new contact, someone the owner's own Ghost won't handle. It makes little sense for existing friends, and that is the owner's call.
