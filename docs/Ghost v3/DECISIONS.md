# Decisions

Locked decisions, newest last. Changing one means a new entry that supersedes it.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why |
|----|------|----------|-----|
| D-001 | 2026-11-08 | Design first: no v3 code until the session docs are agreed. | v2 drifted from its docs. |
| D-002 | 2026-11-08 | A Ghost is someone (its subject), never an assistant. Control lives in the app and lab, not in chat. | Keeps identity and control cleanly separate. |
| D-003 | 2026-11-08 | Layering is Ghost + Persona + Override. | Omar talks differently to different people while staying himself. |
| D-004 | 2026-11-08 | Persona templates hold style only, never facts. Facts live in the Ghost's memory with audience rules. | Lets personas be shared across Ghosts without leaking facts. |
| D-005 | 2026-11-08 | Multiple Ghosts per app; subjects can be the owner, another real person, or fictional. Omar's Ghost is the main one. | "I am immortal. Everyone is." |
| D-006 | 2026-11-08 | One mind per Ghost across all conversations and platforms. | He links chats the way Omar would. |
| D-007 | 2026-11-08 | Ghost is always awake: reads feeds, has moods, sleeps, evolves, shapes his own memory. Locked memories cannot be rewritten by him. | Alive, not just event driven; locked core keeps him anchored. |
| D-008 | 2026-11-08 | Owner chooses which contacts Ghost takes over (or all), can reply manually any time, and Ghost resumes by reading what was said. | Owner and Ghost share the same accounts. |
| D-009 | 2026-11-08 | Platform order: WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat. | Omar's actual usage. |
| D-010 | 2026-11-08 | Phase one: no limits, no AI disclosure, unofficial clients allowed. | Build the imagined Ghost first, restrict later. |
| D-011 | 2026-11-08 | Aliveness runs on cheap logic; model calls only when worth it. | Budget. Carries over v2's pulse idea. |
| D-012 | 2026-11-08 | Accounts are separate from Ghosts. Per contact on an account, the owner assigns Ghost + Persona + Overrides. A dedicated number can be fully assigned to one Ghost. The app itself is a channel. | Covers both "different Ghosts on my number" and "a Ghost with his own number". |
| D-013 | 2026-11-08 | Three tiers of thinking: reflex (pure logic), instinct (fast structured decisions with confidence), thought (full model). Low confidence escalates up. | Aliveness on a small budget; replaces v2's math-only pulse with a smarter cheap middle layer. |
| D-014 | 2026-11-08 | Ghosts can talk to each other. | Nothing stops it in the real world either; a loop brake handles the failure case (Q-011). |
| D-015 | 2026-11-08 | Outreach runs on Pulse + Heartbeat (v2's skeleton) with four fixes: (1) Pulse reads **signals** (items with a reason and a strength, written at intake, by drives, or by Ghost himself), not a fixed weighted formula; (2) drives (inner stats) live in Pulse and can launch a heartbeat with no contact attached; (3) the heartbeat receives the signal's reason; (4) an instinct gate sits between Pulse and Heartbeat, and Ghost owns thresholds and rates. | v2's formula could not express boredom, buried hot news (one intention added 0.05 against a 0.65 threshold), hardcoded the judgment in weights, and woke Sonnet blind ("threshold_crossed"). Understanding happens once at intake, so cost scales with events, not time x contacts. |
| D-016 | 2026-11-11 | An incoming message is a signal (reply-owed, very strong). One queue for replies and outreach; a strong signal wakes Pulse at once instead of waiting for the next tick. | One mind, not a reply bot plus an outreach bot. Replies and outreach compete fairly, and mood applies to both. |
| D-017 | 2026-11-11 | Feelings have two layers: core affect (mood, energy, social battery) and named feelings (unlimited, Ghost can invent new ones), each with intensity, optional target, cause and baseline. Senses push them, Pulse drifts them over time, Ghost retunes baselines and rates in sleep. Feelings write signals, weigh instinct, and reach the heartbeat with their causes; targeted feelings spill into core affect. | Cheap dials for Pulse, meaning for the model, links across chats, and every reply is explainable in the lab. |
| D-018 | 2026-11-11 | Nothing in Ghost's judgment is a hard block: time of day, last chat, busy, double texting, sensitivity only lower the odds. Senses, signal kinds and signal writers are open-ended. He can text several people at once; signals fade and can come back. Owner takeover (D-008) stays a control rule, not a feeling. | Humans break their own rules; hard blocks are hardcoded judgment. |
| D-019 | 2026-11-11 | Sleep: Ghost decides from his energy; the owner can force sleep or wake from the app. Asleep he doesn't reply and nothing wakes him (for now); on waking he works through the backlog slowly. No naps. Dreaming is one batched pass: consolidate, find missed links, retune, reset. | Cheapest time for heavy thinking, and the safety net for intake. |
| D-020 | 2026-11-11 | Memories fade, never vanish: strength affects ranking and what surfaces unprompted, not existence. Fades slower than a human; outdated facts are superseded in dreams; locked memories never fade; no misremembering; only the owner can truly delete. | RAG solves size; fading solves relevance and focus. |
| D-021 | 2026-11-11 | Parallel heartbeats on one live state, one per contact at a time. A burst from one person is one turn; the reply can be several messages, Ghost decides. | Humans juggle chats; nobody answers 5 messages one by one. |
| D-022 | 2026-11-11 | Tier map as in 01_MIND.md. Intake runs on instinct; dreams catch what it misses. | Cheap by default, thought only where writing or judgment needs it. |
| D-023 | 2026-11-11 | Style = real examples + a measured fingerprint + a few soft traits, at three levels (base, persona, override). Overrides are learned by Ghost and editable by the owner, who can lock fields. The lab scores replies against the fingerprint. | Adjectives make a generic AI; examples and measurable habits make "yeah, this is Omar", and drift becomes visible. |
| D-024 | 2026-11-11 | Every memory has an audience. Default: shareable unless it sounds private. Owner locks are hard; Ghost's guesses are soft. He gossips with close people. | Persona decides how he talks, audience decides what he may say. |
| D-025 | 2026-11-11 | Tools are open-ended: owner adds them, Ghost can ask for and build his own. Web search is free. Full catalog in session 3. | He should grow, not be capped by our first list. |
| D-026 | 2026-11-11 | One identity, several workers (intake, pulse, heartbeat, dreamer) sharing one mind; only the heartbeat speaks. Heartbeat and dreamer use a strong model. No draft-and-fix pass. | Coherent identity, cheap where possible, clear in the lab. |
| D-027 | 2026-11-11 | Intake is split: messages and ended-chat summaries go to the strong model; feeds, news and presence go to instinct and escalate when unsure. Refines D-022. | People's messages are few and matter most; the rest is high volume. |
| D-028 | 2026-11-14 | Zero friction: nothing blocks, a Ghost works from minute one and improves with data (levels 0 to 4). Connecting an account is the import. The app shows completeness per kind of data. Texting real contacts is the owner's call at any level. | Testers judge in minutes; a visibly improving Ghost earns more data. |
| D-029 | 2026-11-14 | Rehearsal mode replaces most of the interview: real replays (reply hidden), gap scenarios, casual fact questions, chosen by Ghost from his weakest data. Started by Ghost or owner, topics skippable, text or voice. Rehearsal answers weigh a bit less than real messages. Shadow mode is optional. | Short, spread out, in the owner's real texting voice; replays train and test at once. |
| D-030 | 2026-11-14 | Deep interview is optional, not default, and redone in the new flow (v2's omar.md is reference only). Must-know before texting: name, age, city, language(s). | Power users can go fast without blocking everyone else. |
| D-031 | 2026-11-14 | The owner can mark a contact as a stranger, set a goal, and let Ghost run free; the goal is a long-lived goal signal. | New people need intent, not history. |
| D-032 | 2026-11-14 | One fingerprint per language; Franco-Arabic is its own language; language choice and mixing are learned per contact; he may text in low-data languages, with the gap shown. | People are different texters in each language, and differ per contact. |
| D-033 | 2026-11-14 | Live data is the main source; exports are the starter. Owner manual replies are the best data. Old data (years, not months) weighs a little less, mildly. No per-chat learning exclusion for now. Contacts' messages are used, tagged by origin; contacts' style is learned too. | History goes stale; live data is current and free. |
| D-034 | 2026-11-14 | Photos come from an upload area (no camera roll). Ghost describes them; per photo or per Ghost they are sendable-only or free, with audiences. Media received is saved to remember, never resent. | Safe default with room to set him free. |
| D-035 | 2026-11-14 | Sources: WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat, Discord, Telegram, uploads, feeds. Discord is also a platform he texts on. Other subjects use the same pipeline (details in session 6). | Users live on different platforms. |
| D-036 | 2026-11-14 | Five kinds of data, each with one test: Me, People, Voice, Taste, Media, with a fixed tie-break order. Ghost files everything; kinds appear only in the completeness view. | Ten kinds overlapped; five obvious buckets leave no one unsure. |
